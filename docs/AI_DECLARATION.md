# Declaration of AI assistance

**Contributor:** Edeh Victor Chinomso  
**Email:** edehvictor715@gmail.com  
**Project:** Comparative Analysis of Selected Optimization Algorithms and Loss Functions for Deep Learning-Based Image Recovery and Medical Image Segmentation

I used OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex while preparing this project and my contribution
to the article. The assistance included explaining mathematical and programming
concepts; suggesting and discussing experimental settings; generating, revising
and debugging parts of the Python code; and helping organise the repository.

I also used these tools to assist with literature searches and reference
formatting, drafting and revising portions of the written work, mathematical
typesetting, grammar, and the preparation of tables, figures and presentations.
AI-assisted checks were used to compare reported values with saved experiment
records and identify inconsistencies. This use extended beyond proofreading.

The experimental values reported in the work came from saved computational
runs, rather than values generated by the AI tools. Chisom carried out the
segmentation runs and supplied the resulting records. AI assistance in
summarising or checking those records does not constitute independent human
validation of the experiments.

I remain responsible for reviewing my contribution, checking the accuracy of
the code, citations and interpretations, and correcting any errors before
publication. This declaration describes my own use of AI; other contributors
should confirm their own disclosures. No AI tool is listed as an author.
